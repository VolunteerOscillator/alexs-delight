## About

Alex's Delight is the crossover between Alex's Mobs and Farmer's Delight — turning the creatures you hunt into meals you cook. Crocodile steaks, moose ribs, komodo dragon skewers and a long list of dishes that give the mobs a purpose beyond drops in a chest.

---

## Features

- Farmer's Delight cooking recipes built around Alex's Mobs drops
- Dozens of new dishes with unique buffs and effects
- New ingredients, condiments and cooking components
- Feast blocks for multi-serving meals at a base or event
- Integrates with the Farmer's Delight cooking pot and skillet workflow

## Installation

1. **Download** the JAR matching your Minecraft version from the table above.
2. **Install a mod loader** — Forge, Fabric or NeoForge for your exact game version.
3. **Place the `.jar`** into your `.minecraft/mods/` folder (create it if it is missing).
4. **Launch** Minecraft using the modded profile.
5. Verify **Alex's Delight** appears in the in-game mods list.

> **Tip:** if the game crashes on startup, you almost certainly mixed a Legacy JAR with a Modern
> Minecraft version, or are missing a required dependency. Check `latest.log` first.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need both parent mods?**
A: Yes — Alex's Mobs and Farmer's Delight are both required.

**Q: Where do I cook these?**
A: In the Farmer's Delight cooking pot and skillet, using standard heat sources.

**Q: Do the dishes give buffs?**
A: Many do — nourishment and effect durations vary by recipe tier.

**Q: Is it safe to add to an existing world?**
A: Yes in most cases — back the save up first, as you should with any mod addition.

**Q: Can I use it in a public modpack?**
A: Yes. Both builds are modpack-ready and require no additional setup.
